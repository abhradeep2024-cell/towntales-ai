## 1. Core premise

Naina, who appears to be an ordinary middle-class woman, is in a one-year contract marriage with Aryan Singhania, the arrogant heir to the powerful Singhania conglomerate. Aryan's mother, Kamini Devi, humiliates Naina and repeatedly reminds her that she does not belong in their wealthy family.

At a high-society party in the Singhania mansion, Kamini throws a cheque worth ₹50 crore at Naina and orders her to leave Aryan's life. Naina does not cry. She laughs, tears the cheque apart, and summons a team of suited executives. They place a red folder on the glass table. Aryan opens it and discovers that Naina has acquired a 51% majority stake in Singhania Industries.

Naina reveals that she is the hidden CEO of the conglomerate's biggest global rival. The woman they treated as powerless now controls their company.

## 2. Main characters

### Naina — the hidden CEO / contract wife
- Public appearance: understated, composed, seemingly middle-class.
- True identity: ruthless, strategic CEO of Singhania Industries' biggest global rival.
- Performance: controlled stillness; no crying or pleading. Her power is communicated through eye contact, timing and restrained confidence.
- Continuity: elegant Indian evening attire; maintain the same face, hairstyle, outfit and accessories across all shots.

### Kamini Devi — the mother-in-law
- Role: wealthy, status-conscious matriarch of the Singhania family.
- Performance: openly contemptuous at first; confidence collapses when she understands the takeover.
- Continuity: opulent traditional Indian attire and statement jewellery; same appearance throughout.

### Aryan Singhania — the husband
- Role: arrogant heir to Singhania Industries.
- Performance: enters angry, then shifts to disbelief and fear when he reads the acquisition papers.
- Continuity: tailored luxury evening suit; same appearance throughout.

### Executive team
- Six men in coordinated tailored black suits.
- Function: arrive on Naina's finger snap and deliver the red acquisition folder.
- Continuity: coordinated, disciplined movement; no individual dialogue.

## 3. 60-second screenplay and shot plan

### SHOT 1 — THE HOOK (0–5 seconds)
**Visual:** Extreme close-up in slow motion. A cheque marked ₹50 CRORE flies through the air and strikes Naina's cheek. The opulent chandelier and party guests remain out of focus in the background. Hold on the impact, then cut to silence.

**Camera:** Extreme close-up; shallow depth of field; brief slow motion on impact.

**Audio:** Sharp paper slap, followed by absolute dead silence.

**Dialogue — KAMINI (arrogant sneer):**  
"Yeh 50 Crore pakad, aur mere bete ki zindagi se nikal ja."

**Meaning:** Take this 50 crore and get out of my son's life.

### SHOT 2 — THE ESCALATION (5–15 seconds)
**Visual:** Low-angle tracking shot. Naina looks down at the cheque, pauses, then slowly raises her head. A cold, controlled smirk forms. Dramatic backlight separates her from the shadowy party hall.

**Camera:** Slow low-angle push-in; keep Naina's expression as the focal point.

**Audio:** Aggressive high-tempo classical violin begins.

**Internal voice-over — NAINA (English):**  
"They thought a contract marriage made me their slave. They forgot who actually wrote the contract."

### SHOT 3 — THE POWER SHIFT (15–30 seconds)
**Visual:** Over-the-shoulder shot as Naina tears the cheque into pieces and lets them fall. She snaps her fingers. Six men in tailored black suits enter in perfect sync, moving through the wealthy guests. Guests turn and step aside.

**Camera:** Start over Naina's shoulder; cut to insert of tearing paper; then a controlled tracking shot of the six executives entering.

**Audio:** Crisp paper tearing; synchronized heavy footsteps echo through the hall. Violin continues building.

**Dialogue — NAINA (calm, cutting):**  
"Pachas crore? Kamini Devi, aapki aukaat bas itni hi hai?"

**Meaning:** Fifty crore? Kamini Devi, is that all your status is worth?

### SHOT 4 — THE REVEAL (30–45 seconds)
**Visual:** The lead executive slams a glowing red folder onto a glass coffee table. Aryan storms into frame, grabs the folder and reads the first page. Insert macro shot of his eyes widening in horror. Rack focus back to Naina's smirk.

**Required readable document detail:**  
SINGHANIA INDUSTRIES — 51% MAJORITY STAKE ACQUIRED

The folder must visually communicate a corporate acquisition document, not a random glowing prop. The 51% figure must be legible in the insert shot.

**Camera:** Fast dolly-in to the folder; close-up on the document; extreme macro on Aryan's eyes; rack focus to Naina.

**Audio:** Heavy glass-table slam; accelerating heartbeat bass under the score.

**Dialogue — ARYAN (voice trembling):**  
"Fifty-one percent... tumne hamare saare shareholders kharid liye?"

**Meaning:** Fifty-one percent... you bought all our shareholders?

### SHOT 5 — THE CLIFFHANGER (45–60 seconds)
**Visual:** Low-angle power shot. Naina steps forward and dominates the frame. Kamini collapses to her knees in the blurred background. Golden light cuts through the mansion's massive glass windows. Naina looks directly into camera for the final line.

**Camera:** Low-angle hero shot with a subtle forward move; maintain Naina's dominance. End on an abrupt cut to black.

**Audio:** Massive cinematic boom and heavy bass drop immediately before the cut to black.

**Dialogue — NAINA (quiet, absolute authority):**  
"Check apne paas rakhiye saasu-maa. Yeh ghar, aur aapki company... ab meri hai."

**Meaning:** Keep the cheque, mother-in-law. This house, and your company... are mine now.

**End card:** None. Cut to black immediately after the final word to preserve the cliffhanger.
